    • GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread computes `alloc_bytes + alloc_bytes_uoh
    • (alloc_limit - alloc_ptr)`.

    • End of background mark calls repair_allocation_contexts(FALSE) and void_allocation, which clears those pointers without subtracting that tail. The counter therefore increases without a new object. Collection counters need not change again because this background collection already started.
